Repair the evidence.

Interpretation

Noise changes the operating regime.

At −5 dB, the strict “primary swap” cohort is small and both target and interferer evidence are weak. Recovery increases sharply at 10–15 dB, where wrong-speaker extraction is identifiable and complementary evidence is usable.

Final evidence package

  • Baseline / ablation: Primary is the main-table single-expert baseline; TF-map remains a disclosed auxiliary control and candidate source; Pool D direct, UD, fixed CSG and selected GNR complete the comparison.
  • Statistics: 95% paired intervals and switch-specific McNemar tests.
  • Efficiency: candidate count, forward passes, RTF, latency and peak GPU memory.
  • Robustness: per-SNR WER, content switch, speaker switch and recovery.
  • Listening: deterministic clean and noisy cases, plus a ready-to-run small listening protocol.

Takeaway

Reliable target-conditioned evidence is a prerequisite for grounded speech generation.

Complementary candidate construction repairs the evidence interface. Constrained grounding limits downstream drift. The demo keeps those two contributions distinct.
